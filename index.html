Book a review of your clinic

The price list names a prescription-only medicine.

Botox is the brand name of a prescription-only medicine. CAP Code rule 12.12 says these can't be advertised to the public, and the ASA's guidance counts naming one on a price list as advertising it.

Wording I'd suggest"Consultation for lines and wrinkles: £50. Treatment options and prices are discussed at your consultation."

Your Google listing is advertising too.

A service called "Botox", or a description that names it, is shown to anyone who searches. The same rule applies there as on your website: CAP Code rule 12.12.

Wording I'd suggestRename the service "Consultation for lines and wrinkles" and describe the consultation, not the medicine.

A discount on a prescription-only medicine.

Posts, captions and bios are all ads under the ASA's rules. Promoting a prescription-only medicine, and especially offering money off it, is what CAP Code rule 12.12 covers.

Wording I'd suggest"Book a consultation this month to talk through treatments for lines and wrinkles."
