JOHN PANTELO · ADVERTISING REVIEW — LOGO KIT
============================================

FOLDERS
01_Full_Logo/horizontal  Symbol beside the name. Use this in most places (website header, letterheads, email signatures).
01_Full_Logo/stacked     Symbol above the name. Use this for square or tall spaces.
02_Symbol_Only           The JP symbol alone. Use it when the name is shown nearby, or where space is tight.
03_Favicon               Browser tab and home-screen icons.
04_Social_Media          Profile pictures (1080x1080), LinkedIn banner, X/Twitter header and link-preview images.

Every logo comes as SVG (vector: scales to any size, best for print and design tools) and PNG (transparent background).
The lettering is converted to shapes, so no fonts need to be installed.

WHICH COLOUR VERSION TO USE
navy-lavender    Main logo. White or light backgrounds.
white-lavender   Navy, dark or photo backgrounds.
navy / ink / black / white   One-colour versions for single-colour printing, stamps, embroidery or watermarks.

TIPS
Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the diamond.
Don't stretch, recolour outside the brand colours, or add effects.
Smallest size: full logo 140px wide on screen (35mm in print); symbol only 24px.
