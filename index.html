The rule

Botox is a prescription-only medicine, and UK law bars advertising one to the public. The ASA counts a price list, a Google service and an Instagram offer as advertising, so one word can be enough.

Is this legal advice?

No. Each item links to the published guidance it relates to, and you decide what to change.

Will you report my clinic to anyone?

No. I have no connection to any regulator, and I don't share what I find with anyone.

Will new wording affect my Google rankings?

It can. Taking product names out may reduce traffic for those names, and I can't promise any ranking outcome.

Isn't the ASA's Copy Advice service free?

Yes, and it's good. It checks copy you send in. I go through your website, Google listing and Instagram for you and hand back wording ready to use.
