Is this legal advice?

No. Each entry shows the published guidance it relates to, with a link, and you decide what to change. A solicitor can use the report as a starting point.

Will changing the wording affect my Google rankings?

It can. Taking product names out may reduce search traffic for those names, and I can't promise any ranking outcome. The suggested wording focuses on what patients look for, like lines and wrinkles.

Will you report my clinic to anyone?

No. I have no connection to the ASA, CAP or the MHRA, and I don't share what I find with anyone.

What I collect and where it comes from

Business contact details that clinics publish on their own websites, such as a clinic's general email address and company name. The content of emails you send me. For paying clients, invoicing details and the public pages and posts reviewed.

Why I use it

To send a single business email to a clinic about the advertising on its own website, to deliver reviews clients have paid for, and to keep tax and accounting records. For outreach, the basis is legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): offering a relevant service to a business, using contact details that business has published. For clients, the basis is the contract.

Your right to object

Reply "unsubscribe" to any email, or write to the address above, and I will not contact you again. Requests are actioned within 5 business days. I keep a short suppression list so the address is not contacted in future.

Patient images

Reviews describe before-and-after photos and testimonials in words. I do not copy or store images of patients.

Who else handles it

My email provider (Zoho) and my payment provider process data on my behalf. I do not sell or share contact details with anyone else.

Where it is held and for how long

Records are held by me in Australia and by the providers above. Outreach records are kept for 12 months. Client and invoicing records are kept for 7 years for tax purposes.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information I hold about you, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
